## What $30 actually buys

Starter is $0 per month and includes $30 of compute, with up to 10 GPUs at once. Past $30, Modal bills your card per second. Set a workspace budget around $25 before the first GPU run.

An L4 with about 8 CPU cores and 16 GB of RAM is roughly $1.30 an hour. $30 covers about 23 hours of that. A T4 is cheaper per hour and slower, so the same credit buys more hours and less work.

The 50 files are about 125 hours of video. At 24–30 fps that is roughly 11–14 million frames:

- Around 200 frames/sec end to end on an L4: about 15–20 GPU-hours. Fits.
- Around 100 frames/sec: about 30–40 GPU-hours. Over the credit.
- A plain one-frame-at-a-time loop: several times that. It will spend the credit before the dataset is done.

Running 10 L4s at once does not multiply the bill. Ten GPUs for 2 hours cost the same as one GPU for 20 hours. It only shortens the wait.

Spend the credit in this order: one parquet shard until the results match a careful FP32 run, then the full fan-out once. Keep the FP32 reference on that one shard. Running the slow reference on all 50 files is what blows the budget.
